# Partner Onboarding Guide

## Overview

This document outlines the process for onboarding the first 5-10 partners for BoomCard's soft launch in Sofia, Bulgaria. Partners are local businesses (restaurants, hotels, SPAs, experience providers) that offer exclusive discounts to BoomCard subscribers.

## Partner Types

| Category | Examples | Target Count |
|----------|----------|:------------:|
| Restaurant | Fine dining, casual, cafes | 3 |
| Hotel | Boutique, wellness hotels | 2 |
| SPA & Wellness | Day spas, thermal centers | 2 |
| Experience | Escape rooms, tours, cooking classes | 2 |
| Entertainment | Cinema, bowling, amusement | 1 |

## Onboarding Checklist

### Phase 1: Recruitment (Week 1-2)

- [ ] **Identify candidate** — Google rating 4.0+, active social media, 50+ reviews
- [ ] **Initial outreach** — Email/phone using pitch template (see below)
- [ ] **Discovery meeting** — Present BoomCard, understand their needs
- [ ] **Send partnership proposal** — Standard terms, commission structure
- [ ] **Signed partnership agreement** — Digital or physical contract

### Phase 2: Setup (Week 3)

- [ ] **Business verification** — Valid Bulgarian EIK/БУЛСТАТ, active legal entity
- [ ] **Partner dashboard account** — Create account with PARTNER role
- [ ] **Venue profile** — Name, address, photos, description (BG + EN), category
- [ ] **Menu/offers setup** — At least 3 offers with discount percentages
- [ ] **GPS coordinates** — Verify latitude/longitude for sticker scan radius
- [ ] **Operating hours** — Set weekly schedule

### Phase 3: Sticker Deployment (Week 3-4)

- [ ] **Order BOOM stickers** — Based on venue capacity (1 per table/room)
- [ ] **Sticker locations created** — Map tables/rooms in partner dashboard
- [ ] **QR codes generated** — Unique QR per sticker location
- [ ] **Stickers placed** — Physical placement at partner venue
- [ ] **Stickers activated** — Mark as active in system

### Phase 4: Staff Training (Week 4)

- [ ] **Manager briefing** — How BoomCard works, commission, payouts
- [ ] **Staff training** — What to tell customers, how to verify offers
- [ ] **Test transactions** — Staff runs through sticker scan + receipt flow
- [ ] **FAQ document** — Printed cheat sheet for staff (BG)

### Phase 5: Go-Live

- [ ] **Publish venue** — Make visible in app
- [ ] **Publish offers** — Activate all offers
- [ ] **Test end-to-end** — BoomCard team member visits, scans, redeems
- [ ] **Partner confirmation** — Written confirmation they are ready

## Commission Structure

| Plan | Commission to BoomCard | Partner Keeps |
|------|:----------------------:|:-------------:|
| STANDARD (Free) | 0% | 100% |
| PREMIUM (€9.99/mo) | 2% of bill | 98% |
| PLATINUM (€19.99/mo) | 3% of bill | 97% |

- Commission is calculated on the customer's total bill amount when a BoomCard offer is redeemed
- Monthly settlement by bank transfer
- Detailed transaction reports available in partner dashboard

## Partner Dashboard Features

Partners have access to:

1. **Dashboard** — Overview of scans, redemptions, revenue
2. **Offers Management** — Create, edit, pause, delete offers
3. **Sticker Management** — Generate stickers, view scan activity
4. **Analytics** — Views, click-through rates, popular times
5. **Reviews** — View and respond to customer reviews
6. **Settings** — Business info, operating hours, bank details

## Geographic Strategy (Sofia Launch)

Focus on 4 areas for initial density:

1. **Vitosha Blvd & Center** — High foot traffic, tourists + locals
2. **South Park (Южен парк)** — Restaurants, cafes, young professionals
3. **Studentski Grad** — Budget-friendly, high volume
4. **Lozenets** — Upscale dining, SPAs, boutique hotels

Target: At least 2 partners per zone for the initial launch.

# Target Partners Strategy

## Launch Zone: Sofia, Bulgaria

### Geographic Focus Areas

| Zone | Area | Character | Target Partners |
|------|------|-----------|:--------------:|
| 1 | Vitosha Blvd & Center | Tourist + business, high foot traffic | 3 |
| 2 | South Park (Южен парк) | Young professionals, trendy restaurants | 3 |
| 3 | Lozenets | Upscale dining, SPAs, boutique hotels | 2 |
| 4 | Studentski Grad | Budget-friendly, high volume | 2 |

## Partner Selection Criteria

### Must-Have
- Google rating **4.0+** (minimum 50 reviews)
- Active business with valid Bulgarian EIK/БУЛСТАТ
- Physical location in Sofia (not delivery-only)
- Willing to offer minimum **10% discount** on at least 1 item/service

### Nice-to-Have
- Active social media presence (Instagram 500+ followers)
- TripAdvisor/Booking.com presence
- Multiple locations (expansion potential)
- Experience with loyalty programs
- English-speaking management
